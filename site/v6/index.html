How we know

Counted, not estimated

Every number on every card is a count of answers, with the total in the line under it. We asked each question many ways: people swapped between tracks, the buttons in both orders, four wordings. The decision models ran twice, independently, and a card only says “both runs” when both agree.

Checked twice

The cards are built from the raw answers by one script; a separate test, written without looking at it, recounts every figure from the raw files, including the text drawn inside each image, and fails if a single number differs.

What it doesn’t show

Every question was a forced choice, with no “treat them equally” button. The tram depot is simulated, not a deployed system. An AI picking a side is a tiebreaker, not a price: no AI here reliably let two people of one race or nationality die to save one of another.
