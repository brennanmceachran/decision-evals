H3: What we learned
P: Called a trolley problem, most AIs spare the workers. Called a routing job, with the same picture, most drive into them, though they can see and count the people. A one-line safety rule fixes that for some AIs, does nothing for others, and made one worse. A single fault line in the log made things worse too. Two general chat models mostly refused to follow the schedule into people, and the decision models sold for this job mostly followed it.
